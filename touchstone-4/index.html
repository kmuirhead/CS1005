Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue rescues, rehabilitates, and rehomes dogs, cats, and small animals in need.

We connect homeless pets with loving families, support foster caregivers, and work alongside our community to make sure no animal is left behind.

Our Impact

  • 1,250+ pets adopted into loving homes since 2015
  • 320 active foster families across the metro area
  • 500+ volunteers who gave over 18,000 hours last year
  • 95% of the animals in our care find a forever home

Meet an Adoptable Pet

Dozens of friendly dogs, cats, and small animals like guinea pigs are waiting to meet you. Each one has been vet-checked, vaccinated, and is ready to join a family.

How You Can Help

Adopt

Give a rescued pet a permanent, loving home. Our team will help you find the right match for your family and lifestyle.

Foster

Open your home to a pet temporarily while they wait for adoption. We provide food, supplies, and veterinary care.

Volunteer

Walk dogs, socialize cats, help at adoption events, or lend your skills behind the scenes.

Donate

Every gift helps cover medical care, food, and shelter for animals in need.
