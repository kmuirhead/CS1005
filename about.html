About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2015 when a small group of neighbors started fostering stray pets in their own homes. What started as a few rescues a month has grown into a community-powered nonprofit with hundreds of foster families and volunteers across Minneapolis, St. Paul, and the surrounding suburbs.

Today we continue to rely on the generosity of our community to give every animal a safe place to land and a hopeful path to a forever home.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: Every animal is treated with kindness, patience, and respect.
  • Trust: We are transparent with adopters, donors, and partners about how we work.
  • Community: Rescue is a team effort, and everyone has a role to play.
  • Action: We act quickly so that pets in need get help when they need it most.
